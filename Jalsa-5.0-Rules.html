General Rules

These apply to every event, for every participant.

  1. All participants must register before the registration deadline.
  2. Participants should report at least 15 minutes before their event.
  3. The organizers and referees have the final authority on all decisions.
  4. Unsportsmanlike conduct, abusive language, or cheating will result in disqualification.
  5. Participants must play only in their registered age category.
  6. Any damage to equipment or venue due to negligence will be the participant's responsibility.
  7. In case of rain or unforeseen circumstances, organizers may modify or reschedule events.

How we keep it fair

  • All heats, brackets, pairs and teams were drawn at random by computer. Nobody was placed or paired by hand.
  • If a category has fewer than 3 entries, it will be merged into the next age category. If merging is not possible, the event will still be held but only a First Prize will be awarded. This will be announced before the event starts, not after.
  • Where an event needs partners or teams, they have been paired at random to give everyone a fair chance. Swaps are allowed only before that event's first match, and only with the moderator's approval.
  • Please be on time. Your name will be called once when your heat or match is due. After that call and a short wait, the moderator may award a walkover to your opponent.

Carrom

Adult: 9.30 AM Onwards / Kids: 12:30 PM Onwards Club House

How it is playedDoubles knockout. Partners are paired at random so everyone gets a fair chance. Win and you continue, lose and you are out.

  1. Singles and Doubles are separate competitions.
  2. Knockout. One board per match for kids, best of 3 boards for adults.
  3. Players alternate the break.
  4. The Queen must be covered immediately after pocketing.
  5. Pocketing the striker results in a penalty.
  6. All coins of one colour must be pocketed before winning.
  7. Doubles: partners sit opposite each other and turns rotate clockwise. Partners may not play each other's turn.
  8. Standard International Carrom Federation rules apply wherever applicable.

CategoriesUpto 10 Years — Doubles  ·  10-14 Years — Doubles  ·  Adults — Singles  ·  Adults — Doubles

02

Chess

Adult: 11:30 AM Onwards / Kids: 1:30 PM Onwards Club House

How it is playedStraight knockout. Win and you continue, lose and you are out. If the number of entries does not halve cleanly, one short play-in round is held first, and the entrants not drawn into it wait and join the next round.

  1. Knockout. 10-minute rapid format.
  2. White moves first; colours drawn by the arbiter at the board.
  3. Touch-move rule applies.
  4. An illegal move gives the opponent 2 extra minutes; a second illegal move loses the game.
  5. Checkmate wins immediately.
  6. A drawn game is resolved by a 5-minute blitz game.

CategoriesUpto 12 Years — Singles  ·  12-18 Years — Singles  ·  Senior — Singles

Slow Cycling

2:00 PM B Block, Basement

How it is playedRun in heats, then a final. The winner of each heat goes through. If the final has spare lanes, one extra qualifying heat is run among the runners-up to fill them.

  1. The participant crossing the finish line last wins.
  2. Feet must not touch the ground.
  3. Crossing lane boundaries leads to disqualification.
  4. Pushing or blocking others is prohibited.
  5. A participant who falls or puts a foot down is eliminated.
  6. Tyres must be properly inflated. Letting air out of your tyres to slow the cycle down is not allowed, and a cycle with a tyre below the minimum firmness will not be permitted to start. The moderator checks tyres before the heat.
  7. Heat qualification: the slowest rider still legal in each heat advances. A rider who puts a foot down is out and cannot qualify.

Table Tennis

5:00 PM Club House

How it is playedDoubles knockout. Partners are paired at random so everyone gets a fair chance. Win and you continue, lose and you are out.

  1. Doubles only. Knockout. One game per match.
  2. Match is played to 11 points, win by 2 clear points.
  3. Service changes every 2 points.
  4. Service must bounce once on the server's side and once on the receiver's side, and must be played diagonally.
  5. Partners must strike the ball alternately. Two touches by the same player loses the point.
  6. A let serve is replayed.
  7. Touching the table with the free hand or moving the table during play loses the point.

11

Tug of War

6:00 PM B Block, Basement

How it is playedTeams are drawn at random at the venue, so the sides are balanced rather than chosen. The moderator will explain the running order before you start.

  1. Two categories only: Men's and Women's. There is no block-versus-block contest.
  2. Teams are drawn at random on the day, so the sides are balanced rather than chosen.
  3. Equal number of pullers on both sides. The moderator fixes the number for each bout and counts both sides before the start.
  4. Pullers are rotated between bouts so everyone who registered gets a pull.
  5. The rope marker starts at the centre.
  6. The team pulling the marker across the winning line wins the bout.
  7. Wrapping the rope around the body or hands is prohibited.
  8. Sitting down intentionally is not allowed.
  9. Closed footwear required. No sandals or bare feet.

Skipping

12:00 PM Brandcom Sports Arena

How it is playedEveryone gets a timed attempt and is ranked on the score. No heats, no final.

  1. Time limit: 1 minute. Highest number of successful jumps wins.
  2. Only successful jumps are counted.
  3. The rope must pass under both feet.
  4. Participants may restart immediately after a missed jump; the clock does not stop.
  5. Tie: a 30-second jump-off between the tied participants.

Dodge Ball (Women)

12:15 PM Brandcom Sports Arena

How it is playedTeams are drawn at random and play a round-robin: every team plays every other team once. Most points wins.

  1. Teams of equal size, drawn at random. Round-robin, best of 3 rounds per match.
  2. Players hit by a live ball below the shoulders are out.
  3. Headshots are not counted and the thrower is warned.
  4. If a thrown ball is caught before touching the ground, the thrower is out.
  5. The last team with remaining players wins the round.

Football

1:15 PM Brandcom Sports Arena

How it is playedTeams are drawn at random, then play a knockout — semi-finals and a final.

  1. 5-a-side, knockout.
  2. Match duration: 10 minutes per half.
  3. Kick-off starts each half.
  4. Goalkeeper may use hands only inside the penalty area.
  5. No dangerous tackles. Sliding tackles are not allowed.
  6. Ball crossing the touchline results in a kick-in.

22

Cricket

2:10 PM Brandcom Sports Arena

How it is playedTeams are drawn at random, then play a knockout — semi-finals and a final.

  1. Knockout tournament. Teams of 6-8 players.
  2. 6 overs per innings.
  3. Toss decides batting or bowling.
  4. Each bowler can bowl a maximum of 1 over.
  5. No-ball and Wide each award 1 extra run and an additional delivery.
  6. Free Hit after a front-foot no-ball.
  7. Runs completed before a run-out are counted.
  8. One substitute fielder allowed; the substitute cannot bat or bowl.
  9. Tie: Super Over. If still tied, the team with more boundaries wins.
23

Musical Chair

5:00 PM C Block

How it is playedRun in batches, then a final. The last few standing in each batch go through to the final.

  1. Run in batches, then a final. Chairs = participants minus one in every round.
  2. Participants circle the chairs in one direction only while the music plays. You must keep moving forward.
  3. When the music stops you may only take a chair ahead of you. You cannot turn back or reverse to reach an empty chair you have already passed.
  4. When the music stops, sit down. The participant left standing is out, and one chair is removed.
  5. Holding, pushing or pulling another participant is an immediate elimination.
  6. A participant may not touch or hold a chair while the music is playing.
  7. If two participants land on the same chair, whoever is more fully seated stays; if it cannot be called, both leave and the chair is contested by a re-run between the two.
  8. The music operator must face away from the chairs so that stops cannot be timed to anyone's position.

How it is playedTeams are drawn at random at the venue, so the sides are balanced rather than chosen. The moderator will explain the running order before you start.
